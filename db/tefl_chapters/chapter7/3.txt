**Grammar**

Do you love to diagram sentences and analyze word choice and meaning? Or are you among the many speakers of English who can’t explain the difference between a relative clause and a noun clause? Are you somewhere in between? Regardless of where you stand now, understanding grammar and common difficulties for ESL/EFL students is crucial to becoming an effective teacher.

First, there is too much information about English grammar to be included in this chapter, so for an overview of major grammatical concepts, please see the book, *Grammar Fundamentals for Teaching English as a Foreign/Second Language: A Teacher’s Reference* (Walker et al., 2019). That book was created by ITA instructors in order to provide teachers of English as a foreign/second language with a basic knowledge of a sweeping range of grammatical structures and concepts (or to shore up the previous knowledge that they already have).

With that in mind, the remainder of this chapter considers the classroom application of grammatical concepts, or the why and how of incorporating explicit grammar instruction into our ESL/EFL classes.
