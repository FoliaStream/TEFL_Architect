Dimensions of CULTURE

Brooks Peterson (2004) also used the term “culture scales” to define dimensions of culture, placing each on a scale. Place on the scale does not mean that one culture is better or worse than others, only that it reflects the attitude people in that culture hold.

In this book, we are going to follow Peterson’s scales, but we will only focus on the four categories that have clear implications for the behavior of ESL/EFL students in the classroom. Those categories are displayed in Figure 5.
