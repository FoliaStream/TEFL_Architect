**Stress**

The fifth barrier to cross-cultural communication is stress. Since we do not share the same language, our body movements can be misleading.  
We may have some preconceived beliefs, and may not be aware of different underlying values, which may lead us to becoming stressed and  
irritated. Try to be forgiving of other people’s behavior as well as of your own. Expect ambiguity in international communications and always  
try to maintain a good sense of humor.
