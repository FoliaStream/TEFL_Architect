**Introduction to Writing**

This next section will address the last of the four **language skills**—writing. In language courses, writing is often combined within reading as the skills required to write stem from a range of reading skills, writing is unique to the other skills in a variety of ways. To begin, speaking and listening are that same skills that many people living in contact with other people will develop starting from birth. Writing, on the other hand, is a skill that must be learned (along with reading).

While often considered the most complex skill for students to acquire, writing can be a very rewarding skill to teach as students learn to express themselves through graphic symbols and are able to express themselves in more in-depth and complex ways. Let’s dive in!
