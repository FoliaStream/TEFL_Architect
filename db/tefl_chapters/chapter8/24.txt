**Assessment Tips**

*Ten Listening and Reading Assessment Tips*

1. Make sure the language level (grammatical structures and **levels**) corresponds to your students’ ability.
2. Use text and/or audio of an appropriate length.
3. Use different types of listening tasks based on student need (e.g., monologues, conversations, lectures, TV/radio broadcasts, announcements, interviews, and instructions).
4. Use different types of reading tasks based on student need (e.g., work emails, news **articles**, academic essays, stories, etc.)
5. Wherever possible, use authentic, real-world texts and audio.
6. Choose topics that are relevant and connected to what has been covered in class.
7. Avoid controversial topics or those that require specific background knowledge in order for the text to be understood and questions to be answered correctly and completely.
8. In reading, add paragraph and/or line numbers to facilitate finding answers.
9. If assessing listening, avoid video that contains subtitles, as that also tests reading.
10. Grammar and vocabulary should be tested within the context of a reading or listening.
