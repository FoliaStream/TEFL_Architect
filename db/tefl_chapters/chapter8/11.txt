**Introduction to Reading**

Have you ever caught yourself re-reading the same paragraph several times without actually knowing what you have read? You might wonder what caused this. Maybe you were distracted by background noise or were preoccupied with another thought or worry. Another possibility is that you were reading a subject outside of your area of expertise or experience, causing comprehension problems. A third possibility is that you were simply uninterested in the topic but were obligated to read the material for a class or work assignment (which might be happening to you right now!).

Reading, along with listening, is a receptive language skill, but that does not mean it is a passive skill. While students do not produce language like they do when speaking or writing, their brains are actively working while listening or reading to comprehend the given language input. To help you better understand how your students interact with reading texts, this first section will explore how the brain processes reading.
