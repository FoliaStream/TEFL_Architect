>**CREATING SCAFFOLDED ACTIVITIES IN LESSON PLANS**

**Instructional scaffolding**

Distance / cognitive gap between what child can do unaided and what with help expert 

Support given to learners with intention of helping them achieve
 
Apply scaffolding - help do everything independently:
- teach in steps 
- consider what child do independently 

Teaching with scaffolding - explain go through activity, provide example once feel secure do alone 

Teaching without scaffolding - explained activity and left 


Type of scaffolding: 
- content scaffolding: use familiar / highly interesting material to teach new skills 
- language scaffolding: explicitly teach language used in task 
- task scaffolding: demonstrate task by modeling steps and verbalizing thougth process
- material scaffolding: using written prompts / cues to help students in tasks

>**CONSIDERATIONS IN TEACHING LISTENING / SPEAKING**

**Listening**

Very young / low-level - begin with short situations delivered by familiar source:
- don't expect much - spoken output 
- give encouragement / time / opportunities 

As students grow - develop skills, listen complex situations 

Consider anyways to use visual / gestures 

**Speaking**

Teaching speaking - joys / challenges 

The younger kid = the lower expectation outputs 

Very young - speaking output = listen + repeat 

With more advanced students - focus more on accuracy / fluency 

Nunan - 2 categories speaking activities: 
- *reproductive*:
    - common components of commercial textbooks
    - students reproduce language 
    - situations disconnected from real life 
- *creative*:
    - students use imagination and then speak to achieve goal

>**CONSIDERATIONS IN TEACHING VOCAB / GRAMMAR**

Young learners - vocab / grammar acquisition: learn through exploration of activities centered on themes rather than through explicit focus on metalanguage

Vocab / grammar - interwinde especially with acquisition of language - collocations / chunks of language 

Lower-level -> focus on concrete nouns + repeat a lot to learn 

Make connections between old-new content -> contextualize new vocabulary 

Incorporate new vocab into games 

TPR - also works to expose new vocab / basic grammar without analyze metalanguage 

Advanced learners - learn grammar through context

    - test 

Different distinction:

    - provide way to make adjust to presentation of methods and materials in real time
    - observation/making suggestino of modelling language / note progress overtime 
- Summative:
    - high-stakes assessment 
    - evaluate learn at end of period / more traditional
