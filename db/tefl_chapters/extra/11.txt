>**CULTURE AS AN ICEBERG**

Iceberg - 10% above water and 90% below -> culture is the same:
- surface culture: people's behavior / actions / practices 
- deep culture: attitude / beliefs / values 

Cultural sensitivity - learning to recognize different values lying behind people's words / actions 

---

>**5 BARRIERS TO CROSS-CULTURAL UNDERSTANDING**

Behavior can be different in different countries 

Cultural misunderstanings - barriers: 
- language
- non verbal communication 
- stereotypes
- evaluative tendencies 
- stress 

---

>**LANGUAGE BARRIER**

First barrier - language 

Hard to communicate in host country 

Learn new language - implies learn culture 

EFL class - teach to different cultures -> address differences 

"How are you?" - actual meaning varies in different countries and also how to respond 

---

>**NON-VERBAL COMMUNICATION**

Gestures / way we talk 

Borg - 93% communication from body language (not international)

Common confusing gestures: 
- pointing index finger - rude in asia 
- pointing with foot - very impolite in india
- "come here" with finger - very impolite in japan 
- no/yes with head inverted 
- laugh is embarassment 

---

>**STEREOTYPES**

Collection of attitudes / assumptions about group people 

Negative in general 

Naturally try to classify people in groups 

Stereotypes problems: 
- based on limited experience
- apply entire group but paint only partial image 

Generalizations ≠ stereotypes
    - more accurate
    - based on research 
    - help international communication 

---

>**TENDENCY TO EVALUATE PEOPLE'S BEHAVIOR**

Evaluate good / bad based on own valuaes / standards - not applied to others 

Hofstede - "separate observation from interpretation" - delay interpretation of behavior until know about culture 

Help understand real intention behind actions 

---

>**STRESS**

Difficult communication - stress / irritation 

Try to forgive + have sense of humor 

---

>**DIMENSIONS OF NATIONAL CULTURES**

Structure in variety of cultures 

Hofstede - first notice key points differences 

Peterson - "culture scales": no better/worse, but only reflect attitude of people 

4 categories: 
- Equality / Hierarchy 
- Direct / Indirect 
- Individual / Group 
- Task / Relationship 

---

>**EQUALITY VS HIERARCHY**

Difference towards social status 

- Equality: people equal treatment regardless of position / age / gender 
- Hierarchy: people different role based on position 

Also influence on language used - in English "you" for both formal and informal 

Classroom implications: 
-*Equality*: 
    - student centered 
    - teacher student are equal 
    - students ask / interrupt 
    - students share opinion 
- *Hierarchy*:
    - teacher centered 
    - teacher treated more respect 
    - students don't interrupt 
    - teacher imitates interaction 

---

>**DIRECT CULTURES VS INDIRECT CULTURES**

Direct cultures - express in frank manner, no room for interpretation 

Indirect cultures - diplomatic way to face issues 

Language - direct/indirect degree reflect on language used / strength 

Classroom implications:
- *Direct cultures*:
    - students say clearly if no understand 
    - students express opinion if differ 
    - students enjoy discussing issues 
- *Indirect cultures*:
    - students do not let teacher know if no understand 
    - students do not openly discuss 
    - students avoid issues 

---

>**INDIVIDUAL VS GROUP**

Individual cultures - people as individuals, take initiative, focus on themselves, individual over group, move in-out groups easy 

Group / collective cultures - identity determined by grop, decision made by group, interest group > individual (family >>>)

Classroom implications:

- *Individual cultures*:
    - emphasis learn how to study
    - idea continuous learning 
    - students helping on exam not okay 
    - plagiarism unethical 
- *Group cultures*:
    - emphasis learn how to do things 
    - study only young not adults 
    - let students help / copy 

---

>**TASK VS RELATIONSHIP**

Task style - work very important in life, work > family / leisure

Relationship style - work important, but family >>>

Classroom implications : 
- *Task*:
    - classroom attendance essential 
- *Relationship*:
    - absence for family reasons more accepted 

---

>**CULTURE SHOCK**

Series of stages experienced by person who is immersed in foreign culture:

- Honeymoon stage: just arrived, excited, open to new experience 
- cutlure shock stage: become irritated by misunderstanings, disoriented, miss family friends 
- adjustment stage: recovery adjust to new environment, learn appropriate and inappropriate, accept behavior, make friends 
- reverse culture shock: lonely because family not understand how you changed 
- minimize shock: researhc about country / people / values
