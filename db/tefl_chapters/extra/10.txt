# CHAPTER 10 - Visual Aids & Technology

>**INTRODUCTION**

Use visual aids and technology: 
- facilitate learning process 
- additional way to process info 
- alternative way to expose info 

---

>**RESEARCH**

How vidual aids enhance learning? 
- Well chosen visual aids - authentic language material, better than contrived material designed especially for classroom 
- Students learn using more senses - the more the better, different approach to adjust to different learning processes 
- higher students interest 

Low-tech visual aids:
- require little / no equipment 
- pictures / realia / flashcards / graphic organizers / cartoons / boards / others 

Technological tools:
- visual / auditory / both 
- require special equipment (computers/internet/projectors/dvd players)

---

>**CHOOSING VISUAL AIDS**

Choose according to studs background knowledge / context - cultural taboos to be considered *cultural sensitivity*

To avoid:
- violence
- small images
- poor reproductions 
- stereotypes 

---

>**LOW TECH VISUAL AIDS**

- **Word walls**

Word walls featuring key vocabulary terms 

Very cheap and simple 

Categories of word walls: high-frequency words / literature based / content area 

Word wall - interactive: add words on the way 

Display goals - appealing, easy to process 

- **Magazine cut-outs**

Collect pictures from magazines before-hand to ensure supply 

Pictures - can inspire creativity: make up story starting from picture, can be good to teach new words 

"Guess the picture activity" - ask question related to picture: describe + stimulate creativity

- **Realia** 

Items of everyday life 

Represent tool to illustrate vocabulary taught 

- **Flashcards**

Contain words and pictures 

Example - story in 3 boxes given, describe what is happening / make up story 

Idea - give flashcards to students, put sutdents in pairs, make story 

- **Graphic Organizers**

Table / Venn diagrams / charts / timelines 

Cluster diagrams - brainstorm ideas 

- **Cartoons**

Effective for beginning lesson - illustrate idiom / words 

- **Facial Expressions**

Facial expression / physical movement - promote comprehension 

---

>**TECHNOLOGY: THE RISE IN ONLINE INSTRUCTION**

Technology evolution - impact on TEFL profession 

Many more online tools - supplement / replace f2f teaching 

- **Blended learning**

Approach combines traditional class teaching and online tools 

Create hybrid / blended experience 

F2F classes + computer activities / material 

- **Synchronous classes**

Entirely online 

Both teacher and student online at same time requirement 

Video calls / phone calls / chat / conference 

-**Asynchronous classes**

Entirely online 

No requirement on both online 

Pre-developed courses - content / modules students go through without real-time assistance of teacher 

Operate with LMS (learning management system platforms) / emails / others 

*PROS & CONS of Online Teaching / Learning*
- *Pros*
    - offers location / time convenience 
    - learning anywhere 
    - more class options available 

- *Cons*
    - F2F more familiar / comfortable 
    - more difficult build relationship 
    - tech difficulty / lag time 
    - difficult communication 
    - social benefits of F2F interaction with peers 

---

>**TECHNOLOGY: INTEGRATING TOOLS IN CLASSROOM**

Access to technology - many diverse options 

Technology does not replace good teaching methodology 

Students do not automatically know how to use technology 

Very important to careful plan use of tech tool 

Terminology: 
- CALL - computer assisted language learning (CDs, podcasts, webpages, others)
- MALL - mobile assisted language learning 
- CMC - computer mediated communication 
- Podcasting - download audio / video segments 
- Video chats - can be used to integrate guest spakers from anywhere
- Blogs - writing online to share experience 
- Wikis - writing posted allow multiple users to add content / edit word / keep record of modification
