**TEFL as a Profession**

The field of TEFL has changed a lot over the past few decades. What was once a disorganized and disbanded group of ex-pats, working with few resources and lacking professional support, has now morphed and expanded into a formal field of work and study. EFL teachers can and should view themselves as professionals in an academic field, and as members of that field, it is important to learn and use the appropriate terminology. This includes the common terms about the profession (with numerous acronyms), as well as those about the learners and their level of proficiency with the language.
