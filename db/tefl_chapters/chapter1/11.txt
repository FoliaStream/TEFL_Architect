**Balancing New Responsibilities**

Starting any new position can be stressful, and doing so in a new country/culture adds to the potential for even more challenges. It can be difficult to handle stress in this new situation in which you are a manager and a role model. How can we make our lives easier? It is key to be intentional about being organized, making time for leisurely activities, and taking care of yourself. Many teachers and websites will recommend that you organize yourself inside and outside of work. The more organized you are, the easier the job will be. If you commute to work on the train or bus, bring papers to grade or resources for lesson planning. While you should allocate time to plan and grade at home, you also need to fit in fun activities. Plan to exercise each day to stay in shape. Plan when you will take time for leisure activities, like playing sports or watching TV or a movie. See when you can leave town and explore your surroundings. Meditation is also a good method of relaxation that can allow you to “unplug” from everything. Many people join unofficial clubs or sports teams to relax and unwind. These things can help you maintain balance in your life as a teacher and avoid any form of burnout.
