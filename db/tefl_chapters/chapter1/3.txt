**Languages of the World and English**

It is estimated that there are between six and seven thousand languages spoken around the world today. Each of these languages belongs to a family group of historically related languages.

English belongs to the Indo-European language family. The closest related language to English is Dutch, followed by Danish and German. To compare how closely related these languages are, notice some of the linguistic similarities in the following sentences:

- *English*: I will never see you again.
- *Dutch*: Ik zal je nooit meer zien.
- *Danish*: Jeg vil aldrig se dig igen.
- *German*: Ich werde dich nie wieder sehen.
