**Language of the Profession**

As with most fields, there is a long list of terms unique to the world of teaching EFL/ESL. Here are a few terms, along with their abbreviations and short definitions. Online resources are also provided below.

- English for Academic Purposes (EAP): A subcategory of English for Specific Purposes, EAP programs focus on vocabulary and language skills needed to succeed in an all-English academic environment.
- English Language Learner (ELL): This term is used to refer to any student learning English as a second (or third, fourth, etc.) language.
- English as a Foreign Language (EFL): When we teach EFL, we are teaching English to students in their native country. An English teacher instructing native Korean students in Korea is an EFL instructor. Many online teachers that work with students abroad are also EFL instructors.
- English as a Second Language (ESL): This term commonly refers to teaching English to non-native speakers within a country where English is the main language spoken. We would use the term "ESL" when teaching English in the USA to students who are from Poland, China, Mexico, or another country whose official language is not English.
- English for Specific Purposes (ESP): ESP refers to any English curriculum designed for a specific industry or trade. For instance, there are English classes for nurses, as well as curricula designed for the hospitality industry.
- L1: This refers to a student's primary language.
- L2: This refers to the language the student is learning.
- TL: This refers to the student's target language, or the language being learned.
- Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL): This refers to the field of professionals who teach English in a country where English is not the primary language or online English teachers.
- Teaching English as a Second Language (TESOL): This refers to the field of professionals who teach English in a country where English is the primary language.
- TESOL International: This is the name of the international organization of TESOL instructors.
- Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL): The TOEFL, administered by the Educational Testing Service (ETS), is a required exam for admission into many universities in the United States and elsewhere and is designed to assess international students' level of English for academic purposes.


*TESOL: The definition was originally "Teaching English as a Second Language." However, as researchers and teachers in the field reflected that many people who were learning English already speak more than one language, the definition was changed to "Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages."
