**Blogs**

Assigning blog writing to students as an ongoing class project has a lot of advantages. Students will likely improve their writing fluency, learn a lot about each other, and have a chance to interact and support each other outside of the classroom. Daniele Munca (2007) gives instructions on how students can create their own blogs, recommending that students be required to post a paragraph weekly and comment on two of their classmates’ blogs weekly as well. Students can even post photos on their blog to illustrate a story, such as what they did over the weekend or what their honeymoon looks like. As the teacher, you can make this assignment as focused or as flexible as you wish. It is recommended that you investigate privacy issues and discuss privacy options with students and administrators before making this a classroom requirement.
