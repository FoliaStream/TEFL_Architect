**Introduction**

At this point in the coursebook, you should understand (if you did not already) that learning an additional language in the classroom can be a complex and lengthy process. You learned in chapter 3 that there are various methods you can use to facilitate this process, and you learned in chapters 6-9 that language learning can be broken into various components (grammar, vocabulary, skills, etc.).

This chapter introduces yet another consideration to facilitate language learning for your students – the use of visual aids and technology. Appropriate use of these tools can enhance your lesson plans and give students additional ways to process information. Incorporating visual aids and technology also provides alternative ways to expose learners to the content in your lessons.

The goal of this chapter is to give you practical ideas for incorporating visuals and technology into your lessons, thereby encouraging you to reflect on your plan and consider what, if any, visuals and tech could be appropriate additions.
