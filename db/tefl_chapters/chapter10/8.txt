**Internet as an Educational Tool**

In general, the internet can be used in and out of class for lessons targeting listening, reading, and research skills. The possibilities are only limited by the teacher’s creativity and available resources. The following sites provide examples of web-based learning activities and tools:

- [ ] Essential Technology For Project-Based Learning from Tech&Learning  
- [ ] 22 Free Tech Tools For Teachers from EdApp  

Teachers can make their own online quizzes free of charge through programs such as *Hot Potatoes*, *Google Forms*, or *Quicket*. For visuals, *Pixabayls* one easy way to find images online for almost any subject.

Numerous websites are designed for **ESL/FEL** learners, providing a wide array of exercises. You will find listening comprehension activities with interactive quizzes, as well as grammar and vocabulary lessons accompanied by quick assessments for students to check their answers. As with any teaching material, examine the activity carefully before assigning it, and make sure that students have enough information to complete the **task**.
