**Role Plays**

A role play is an activity in which a student acts out the role of a different person and solves a real-life problem or completes a real life task. Role-play tasks can be effective with adult learners, and even simplified role-play tasks can be beneficial for young learners above the age of five. Assuming an imagined identity helps many students feel less inhibited and makes them more talkative. Examples of role-play cards are shown in Figure 5 and Figure 6. When designing a role play, make sure you give each student a card with specific information to guide students’ acting. You can make different role cards for different pairs/groups and later have students switch cards with other groups. After students have practiced the dialogues in their pairs/groups, they present their sketches in front of small groups or the whole class.
