**Authentic Materials**

Unlike coursebooks and other teaching aids that were created for EFL students, authentic materials consist of resources that are used in English-speaking countries by native speakers. Examples include newspapers, books, brochures, leaflets, menus, tickets, bank cards, library cards, etc.

An example of a good EFL activity using authentic materials is acting out a skit in a restaurant. Before the students can act it out, you need to download and print a menu from a real restaurant in the U.S. and introduce the menu vocabulary, useful phrases for ordering meals in a restaurant, and phrases used to take customers’ orders. Afterward, students can role play from a script (for beginners) or create a skit (as a more advanced activity); one student plays the waiter and others play customers.
