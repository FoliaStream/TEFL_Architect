**The Role of Cooperation and Collaborative Learning**

A student-centered classroom is also a place where students’ collaboration and cooperative learning are encouraged. Jones (2007) notes that students should communicate and cooperate with their peers in order to learn from each other and to be less dependent on the teacher. Other students can be a great source when it comes to explaining some complex grammar structures, new vocabulary items, or different learning strategies.

In cooperative learning, students can use each other’s resources and skills, such as asking for information, evaluating another learner’s work, monitoring group work, etc. This teaches them to take responsibility for their own learning, and it has the potential to prevent many behavioral problems by helping students to get to know – and empathize with – each other.
