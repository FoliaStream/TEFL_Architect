**Scavenger Hunt**

A scavenger or treasure hunt is a game in which students work in teams and search for specific items from a given list. The teacher provides a written list of items (or pictures for very young learners) that can be found in school or outside the school. Students usually have to find the items and either bring them to class (e.g., 5 pencils and 4 leaves of different colors) or take a photo of them (e.g., one member of your group in front of a monument). Young, beginning learners could practice presenting their items orally in a group or in front of the class. For more advanced students, the items could be written in the form of a riddle for more of a challenge. Consider the following two examples:

1. They come in different colors, but the most commonly used ones are black and blue. Teachers use them to write on the whiteboard.
2. They are made of metal and plastic. There are different types for gardening, beauty care, or art projects. The most common ones are about 10 inches long. Teachers and students use them a lot, but be careful! They can be sharp.
