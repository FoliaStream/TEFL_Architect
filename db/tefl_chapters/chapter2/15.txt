**Case Studies**

A case study is an example of a real-life task, such as holding a meeting, negotiating a pay raise, or resolving a problem with an employee. Case studies can be used in any EFL lesson, but they are most frequently used in teaching Business English to adult learners.

First, the teacher introduces the vocabulary and phrases typically used in such a situation. Then, students read an example of how such a situation is typically resolved in the U.S. Finally, students take on different roles and role play a similar scenario. An example of a Business English scenario is presented in Figure 4.

*Figure 4.* Business English scenario

| Scenario: Pierre is the owner of a small tour company called Paris Tours. He has 10 employees, including four supervisors, Kana and Thomas (guides), Mason (driver), and Charlie (receptionist). These four supervisors will be called to a meeting to discuss the upcoming tourist season. |
|---|
| - Pierre will send a reminder in the form of an email to the supervisors. |
| - Kana will write an agenda for the meeting. |
| - Thomas will run the meeting. |
| - Charlie will take minutes. |
| - Mason will recap and close the meeting. |
