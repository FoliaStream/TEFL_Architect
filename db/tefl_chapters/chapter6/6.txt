**Word Roots, Prefixes, and Suffixes- Practice**

**Table 1** gives some examples of prefixes and suffixes. Brainstorm your own examples in the last column and check your **accuracy** with a dictionary.

*Table 1*. Practice with suffixes and prefixes

| Description    | Example   | Used in a word |
|---|---|---|
| Prefixes that mean *not* or *absence of*    | a- un- non- in- | amoral uncommon noncommittal inconsistent |
| Prefixes that mean *under*    | sub- under- | submarine understatement |
| Suffixes that usually indicate the word is an adjective | -able -ial -ous | suitable territorial dangerous |
| Suffixes that usually denote a noun    | -ogy -age -cy | biology courage infancy |
| **Suffix** that usually indicates word is an adverb    | -ly    | kindly |
