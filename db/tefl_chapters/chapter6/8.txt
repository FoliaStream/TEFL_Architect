**Adjectives and Adverbs**

There are also common collocations of adjectives + nouns and adverbs + adjectives:

- fast food (not "quick" food)
- junk food (not "trash" food)
- health food (not "well-being" food)
- blissfully ignorant (not "ecstatically" ignorant)
- hopelessly lost (not "downheartedly" lost)

**Specific Order**

Zimmerman (2009) also suggests that teachers make students aware that some collocates must occur in a certain order:

- Back and forth (not forth and back)
- Life and death (not death and life)
- Sink or swim (not swim or sink)
