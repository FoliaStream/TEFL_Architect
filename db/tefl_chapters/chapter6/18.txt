**Communicative Output**

It is important to provide input that includes target vocabulary within a meaningful context, but it is also important (while perhaps more difficult) to create output-based tasks that require the use of specific **lexis**. All types of lesson plan formats will need to include this in order to be successful, and within our PPP lesson plan format, we should be pushing for our students to use language communicatively in the **production stage**, orally and/or in writing. Here are a few ideas for production tasks that include a vocabulary component:

- Assigning students to use the words in an original written role-play. Different groups can be assigned different contexts in which to use the words so that all the students can be exposed to the words in multiple contexts (see Herrel and Jordan, 2004, p. 161).
- Group ordering tasks, in which small groups are given a list of target items and told to negotiate a ranking of importance (for example, given a list of ten common items, students must agree on which five to bring on a camping trip)
- Personal paragraphs explaining the students' own connection with the **lexis**
- Vocabulary **journals**
