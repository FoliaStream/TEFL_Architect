**Introduction**

One of the defining features of any language, including English, is its **lexis**, which is a broad term for vocabulary and its various facets. Naturally, students must acquire vocabulary to understand English when listening and reading and to be understood in English when speaking or writing. When teaching vocabulary, you are not only asking students to learn new words, but also teaching them strategies to continue to learn new words. As Richards & Willy state (in Nation, 2002), "[w]ithout an extensive vocabulary and strategies for acquiring new vocabulary, learners often achieve less than their potential and may be discouraged from making use of language learning opportunities around them" (p. 255).

Various strategies for teaching and learning lexis will be covered in the second half of this chapter, as will the assessment of vocabulary. First, we should consider some of the important features and subsets of English lexis.
