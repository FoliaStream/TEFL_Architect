**Lexis**

Knowing basic concepts about parts of speech (nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc.) will help you to better guide your students, and the part of speech of English are considered extensively in the book titled *Grammar Fundamentals for Teaching English as a Foreign/Second Language: A Teachers Reference*. However, there are many facets of texts that are not covered in that text but are important for English teachers to understand. These include such things as word roots, suffixes/prefixes, idioms, **collocations**, phrasal verbs, and various word associations.

Use of this terminology with your students will depend on the level, age, and needs of each class. For example, for intermediate learners and beyond, exercises that promote awareness of word roots, prefixes, and suffixes may be appropriate. In any case, before discussing how we should teach Lexis in the classroom, we should first consider some of the various features of English vocabulary that are important for teachers to understand.
