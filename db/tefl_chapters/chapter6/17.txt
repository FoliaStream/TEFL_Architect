**Controlled Exercises**

Once these words have been initially presented, various tools can be used to reinforce targeted vocabulary. These tools to reinforce vocabulary include but are not limited to:

- Two to three sentences featuring the target words in order to give them additional context; these can be read for comprehension, or the words themselves can be blanked out, creating a cloze (fill-in-the-blank) activity
- Matching words with images or definitions
- Sorting or categorizing words into recognizable groups
- Providing synonyms and antonyms to words
- Collocation tasks (matching target words with other words/phrases with which they naturally co-occur)
