Alternatively, many language textbooks are organized by themes or general topics, which are followed by the other contents of each unit. **Figure 4** displays an example of a beginner-level textbook that is organized in this way. The units are defined primarily by theme (e.g., *Unit 2: The classroom*) and topics within that theme (e.g., *What we do in class, Where things are, etc.*). The grammar and skills practice come after and complement these themes and topics. More that most textbooks for younger learners are organized in this way because explicit grammar terms (such as *present simple*) are not commonly used with children, and books that are organized by themes are easier and more enjoyable for children in general.
