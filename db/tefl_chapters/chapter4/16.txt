**Language Production**
With very young learners under the age of 5, the level of production you see in a lesson will vary from limited to nonexistent, depending on student age and development. If you choose to use the PPP template as a starting point, consider revising the basic format and creating many short practice activities with no production expectations or possibly only one very simple production activity that avoids written production and focuses on speaking production at the word or phrase level. See Appendix C to view a sample young-learner lesson plan.

Songs are also common tools in the young learner classroom. With the right topic and careful consideration to age and lyrics, songs can be used with your youngest learners all the way up to your oldest. With very young learners, you will want to make sure that they incorporate movements that match the lyrics. You will also want to be sure to select songs that have limited repetitive vocabulary. **Out-of-Class Workload**
Homework might not be appropriate for all young-learner classrooms. In some cases, your students may be in rigorous school systems and already have a great deal of daily homework to complete. Other students may simply not be of an appropriate age for homework. By contrast, some schools may require you to give set assignments and yet others may expect you to create your own. Given this diversity, it is best to check with the school to see if your young learners are expected to complete homework assignments and to what degree.
