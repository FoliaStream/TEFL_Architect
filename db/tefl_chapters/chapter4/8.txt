**Lesson Plan Stages**

There are many lesson plan formats available for use on the internet, and plans may even be provided as part of the teacher's manual of the textbook you are using. While there are many variations of lesson plans and no one accepted format, almost all of them include the basic components of language input, language practice, and student output. One of the most common lesson plan formats is the one known as Presentation-Practice-Production (or PPP). Appendix A at the end of this chapter contains a blank Lesson Plan Template you can use and adapt, and Appendix B provides a sample lesson plan in the PPP format. It is up to you to find a lesson plan format that works best for you, your teaching situation, and the needs and goals of your class and students. A PPP language lesson can be broken down into seven basic stages:

2. Introduction  
3. Presentation
