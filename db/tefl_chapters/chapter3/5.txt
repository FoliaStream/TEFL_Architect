**Technique**

We have established that an approach is a general philosophy that teachers apply to their teaching and a method is a specific structure or plan that educators use to teach. A technique is a specific
tactic used to carry out a chosen teaching method. A technique can sound rather general, despite being for a specific practice. However, ZATEC (2001) suggests the following definition to differentiate
between these three concepts:

"according to Anthony's (1963) model, approach is the level at which assumptions and beliefs about language and language learning are specified; method is the level at which theory is put into
practice and at which choices are made about the particular skills to be taught, the content to be taught, and the order in which the content will be presented; technique is the level at which
classroom procedures are described." (p. 5)
