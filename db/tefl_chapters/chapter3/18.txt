**Suggestopedia Method**

_Suggestopedia_ is one of the humanistic methods that is by far the most different from western mainstream language teaching. This method is directly connected to the idea of lowering student inhibitions. While Suggestopedia is mostly associated with adult learners, young learners in the early developmental stages of life are equally, if not more, affected by inhibitions than older learners (Brown and Lee, 2015, p.113). Thus, lowering inhibitions is important for language learners of all ages. With regards to Suggestopedia, there are many definitions. Nieves (1995) defines the method as follows:

"[A] humanistic foreign teaching method that uses a combination of **technique**, such as physical relaxation and visualization exercises, affirmations and relaxing music based on suggestive principles to strengthen self-esteem and expand the learner's memory capabilities, enabling students to learn with greater speed, little conscious effort and a great amount of pleasure." (p. 131)

According to Suggestopedia, instructors should be sure to create an environment that is conducive to learning. In an optimal Suggestopedia environment, the room should be painted with colors that invite and entice learners. Artwork should also adorn the walls to optimize learners' comfort. Music is used to relax students and make it easier for them to learn. Nieves (1995) mentions that two types of music should be used: "Baroque music is used with the passive concert for the **presentation** of new information while classical music is used for the active concert" (p. 139). The term "concert" describes the type of information presented by the teacher and corresponds with specific music types to be played in the background.

Teachers are also leaders who make this environment positive for learners (Nieves, 1995). It was thought that through an optimal language learning environment, language learning would occur in the best way. Knowing the pitfalls of Grammar Translation, the **Direct Method**, and the **Audiolingual Method**, many educators saw Suggestopedia as a method that would finally be student-centered and accommodate different learners.

While Suggestopedia is very different from many contemporary teaching approaches, it holds some valuable lessons. The teacher influences the environment for the students. Think about the situation in which you would learn more - in a class with a visibly tired teacher who lacks enthusiasm, or in a class with a teacher who is excited to be there and who draws others into their high energy level? Most would prefer the second classroom environment, so whatever methods you use, think about how you can build yourself up to this level of excitement before every class that you teach.
