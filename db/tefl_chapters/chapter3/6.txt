**Traditional Western Methods to Teaching Languages**

Many of the methods and approaches popularized in Europe and North America are based on grammar (Omaggio-Hadley 2001, p. 106). The three methods that came first were Grammar Translation,
Direct, and the Audiolingual Method. Each was a reaction to the pitfalls of the previous method. At the time each method was popular, they were streamlined to work with the situations at hand, but
over time attitudes changed, and people looked for other options. One thing to note is that any method that has been used in the past can work for some people in the present. In fact, if you open any
ESL/EFL or foreign language textbook, you will still find pieces of these methods in them today.
