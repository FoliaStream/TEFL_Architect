**Direct Method**

The *Direct Method* involves instructors teaching language by directly associating words to pictures or objects in the language being taught. Watch the following video to see a sample of the Direct Method with young learners:

The Direct Method allows for quite a bit of language to be learned in a short period of time. It is especially effective with young learners who are not developmentally ready to move beyond concrete ideas and events. A disadvantage of this method, however, is that there is no way to teach actions or abstract ideas. Total Physical Response (**TPR**), which you will see later in this chapter, was created as an extension of this method in order to incorporate more actions and activities.
