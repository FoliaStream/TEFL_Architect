**Approach**

A teaching approach is a general philosophy that you use to plan classroom activities and carry them out. For example, if you hold to the behavioral theory of learning, you believe that students learn
based on a reward and punishment system, used to either reinforce or discourage certain behaviors. In psychology, BF. Skinner was a proponent of the behavioral approach. Noam Chomsky, however,
saw language learning in a dramatically different light. He believed that our brains are hard-wired to acquire grammar and language from birth and that reward and punishment have little to do with
acquiring language. You will find that you can use more than one teaching approach and even come up with your own personal approach. Your approach will probably be a mix of different approaches
based on your teaching experience and what you have learned in other classes, as well as your independent learning activities in life.
