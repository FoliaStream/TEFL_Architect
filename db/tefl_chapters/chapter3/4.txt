**Method**

If an approach is a general philosophy behind your teaching, a method is the way that you plan to implement this teaching philosophy (Richards & Rodgers, 1987). An approach is your general
philosophy, whereas a method is how you concretely plan to teach students. For example, being a vegetarian is a philosophy of not eating foods derived from animal products. However, there are
different ways that people implement this: vegan, which has no animal products, or ovo-lacto, which allows for milk products and eggs. Vegetarianism is the approach to eating, whereas veganism
and ovo-lacto are the methods that people use to implement their philosophy of vegetarianism.
